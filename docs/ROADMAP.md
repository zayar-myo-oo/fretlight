# Focus

The product loop is: see a note → hear it → understand its relationship → play it.

## Current

- Interactive 2D fretboard with audible notes, scales, chords and interval labels.
- Relationships view for selected notes, keys and chord tones.
- Microphone/interface listening for single notes or chords.
- Compact metronome, tuner and instrument settings.
- Local audio takes with timestamped note/chord transitions, synchronized replay, export and session history.

## Next

- Validate note and chord recognition against recorded and live instruments.
- Measure response time and reliability across phones and audio interfaces.
- Add strum onset and beat alignment feedback within the fretboard.
- Evaluate rhythm-pattern recognition on real strumming recordings.

Downstroke/upstroke classification is not currently implemented. Live chord
recognition does not establish strumming direction or exact string/fret position.
Camera tracking, 3D, song analysis, generated ideas, loop recording and separate
training dashboards are outside the current product scope.
